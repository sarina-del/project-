Human judgement still matters

This tool supports awareness, but it does not replace human judgement or professional fact-checking.

Verification is important

Users should compare information with trusted sources before sharing it online.

Education, not censorship

The aim is to educate users and encourage safer digital decisions, not to censor opinions.
